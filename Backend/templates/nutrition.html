HEALTHY EATING GUIDE

A healthy diet is an important part of your fitness journey. Choose nutritious foods, eat balanced meals and stay properly hydrated.

🥗 WHAT SHOULD YOU EAT?

Choose a variety of nutritious foods for a balanced diet.

🍎 Fruits

Eat fruits such as apples, bananas, oranges, guava, papaya and other seasonal fruits. Fruits provide vitamins, minerals and natural carbohydrates.

🥦 Vegetables

Include green leafy vegetables, carrots, tomatoes, beans, broccoli and other vegetables.

🌾 Whole Grains

Choose oats, brown rice, whole wheat roti, jowar, bajra and other whole grains.

🫘 Protein Foods

Include dal, beans, chickpeas, soy products, paneer, milk and curd as suitable protein sources.

🥜 Nuts & Seeds

Almonds, peanuts, walnuts and seeds can be included in appropriate portions.

🥛 Dairy Foods

Milk, curd and paneer can be part of a balanced diet if they suit your dietary preferences.

✅ HEALTHY EATING HABITS

Simple habits that can support your fitness journey.

🥗 Eat Balanced Meals

  • ✓ Include vegetables
  • ✓ Include fruits
  • ✓ Include protein foods
  • ✓ Choose whole grains

🍳 Choose Healthy Foods

  • ✓ Prefer home-cooked food
  • ✓ Eat different food groups
  • ✓ Choose nutritious snacks
  • ✓ Limit processed foods

⏰ Regular Eating

  • ✓ Don't skip meals unnecessarily
  • ✓ Follow a regular eating routine
  • ✓ Eat appropriate portions
  • ✓ Listen to your hunger

❌ WHAT SHOULD YOU LIMIT?

Try to limit these foods, especially when consumed frequently.

Foods to Limit

❌ Excessive sugary drinks

❌ Excessive sweets

❌ Frequent fast food

❌ Deep-fried foods

❌ Excessive packaged snacks

❌ Excessive added sugar

❌ Excessively salty foods

❌ Highly processed foods

💧 STAY HYDRATED

Drink water regularly throughout the day. During workouts and hot weather, you may need more fluids because of increased sweating.

💧 Drink water regularly
🏋️ Drink during workouts when needed
☀️ Pay attention to hydration in hot weather
🚰 Keep a water bottle with you

🏋️ FOOD AROUND YOUR WORKOUT

Simple food ideas around your gym session.

Before Workout

Choose an easily digestible meal or snack according to your schedule. Examples include banana, oats, fruit or a light meal.

After Workout

Have a balanced meal containing a protein source and carbohydrates. Examples include dal-rice, paneer with roti, curd with a meal, or another balanced meal.
